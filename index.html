Your progress

How your data is saved

Only in this browser, on this device. Everything you tick or type is saved to your browser's local storage the moment you change it. There's no account and no server. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere, and there's no tracking.

What's saved: your class, faction and main/alt picks; leveling, daily and weekly ticks; item level and gear steps; reset settings and any gates you corrected. Each class keeps its own progress.

What deletes it:

  • Clearing cookies and site data (clearing only history or cache is fine)
  • Cleanup apps and "clear on exit" browser settings
  • Private or incognito windows, when you close them
  • Safari on iPhone and Mac, if you don't visit for 7 days

Other devices don't see it. Your phone, your PC, and even Chrome vs Edge on the same PC each have their own copy.

How backups work: the file is a plain copy of your progress. The link carries the same data after the # in the address. Browsers never send that part to a website, so the Codex server never receives it. Anyone you share the link with can load your checklist, but there's nothing personal in it.

              1. 1
                Fill every character slot and take each to level 10.About ten minutes each once you can skip the cutscenes. Every alt's cube and warehouse is extra storage for crafting materials, and expanding a cube on a fresh alt costs about 120,000 Kinah. On a main past 100 slots it's closer to 10 million. ▶ 1:56
              2. 2
                Planning to play alts? Take them to 22 and park them.Odyle Energy only starts recharging after the level-22 red quest opens Expeditions. From then on it builds up while you play your main (cap 840 with a subscription, 540 without). Level them to 45 later using the banked energy. ▶ 4:24
              3. 3
                Hold the green quest that rewards a cube line.Early on, that line costs about 5,000 Kinah. Abandon the quest now and turn it in once the next line costs millions, because the quest unlocks it for free. Do every other regional green quest after 45. ▶ 12:48

              Leveling to 45

              1. 4
                Rush 45 before the first daily reset.Do only 3–4 sealed dungeons at 22 and again at 33 to fill the Ascension gauge (about 4% each). Skip strongholds, feathers and the rest, or you'll miss the first day of Duty Missions. ▶ 8:39
              2. 5
                Reroll Duty Missions for Hidden Cube keys, not crystals.The first reward slot is guaranteed. A key costs 50 Quna (premium currency) in the shop, while 600 crystals take about ten minutes to farm. Reroll until the key sits in the first slot. ▶ 10:21
              3. 6
                Solo the story dungeons.Bosses get stronger for every extra player. Krao Cave, Urugugu Canyon, Fire Temple and Draupnir are all soloable on any class, and you won't lose time waiting for a group. ▶ 15:32
              4. 7
                Ascension Trial: skip Easy.Save your weekly entry for Normal. He expects the global requirement to be around 1.3–1.4k. The mobs are easy; the item-level requirement is the only hurdle. ▶ 14:26

              Your first gear

              1. 8
                Only spend Odyle Energy on the right chests.Skip the Krao Cave and Urugugu Canyon chests during the story. Open Fire Temple once for a chance at the weapon, and don't farm it. Open Draupnir twice with a subscription or three times without to get the starting set. ▶ 16:22
              2. 9
                Make the Draupnir set your main gear.Run Krao Cave for the accessory (about 7 runs with a subscription). Then farm Draupnir: enchant to +10 and add manastones, but skip soul binding for now. Build power for Urugugu Canyon and Vakron, which are your next stage. ▶ 17:18
              3. 10
                Never dissolve or sell gold items.Using a gold item as the material for a soul-bind reroll gives a 100% chance to reroll the stat you picked. Stockpile them. ▶ 18:33

              Weekly routine

              1. 11
                Buy all 12 Command Merchant contracts a week.Common merchant in your main city, plus a second Command Merchant in the Abyss. The price rises with each contract but the rewards stay the same. Most new players never find it. ▶ 19:13
              2. 12
                Faction artifact: go late in the week.It opens twice a week, only when your side holds the artifact. Enter on the last day, a few hours before reset, when you're at your strongest. The run lasts about 2 minutes, so kill as many mobs as you can for Abyss Points. ▶ 21:47
              3. 13
                Always open Hidden Cubes with a key.They can drop Odyle Energy, stones worth selling, and wings that sell very high in the first weeks. Spend your keys early, while the prices are high. ▶ 22:58
              4. 14
                Nightmare: never let the counter hit 14.Entries recharge twice a day and stop at 14. If you can't kill the last boss yet, farm the easier ones, since repeat kills still pay. Spend the points in the Nightmare trade shop. ▶ 24:58
              5. 15
                Subscribed? Clear the weekly special shop.Go to Shop, then Black Cloud Merchants, then Special, and buy everything (Odyle Energy, Nightmare clear tickets and so on) except the Resurrection Spiritstone, which you'll have plenty of. Also buy the energy in Morph's special section. It resets weekly, so don't skip a week. ▶ 26:29

              Early-game money

              1. 16
                Camp field bosses with friends.Check the spawn timers under Map, then Exploration, then Field Bosses. Their gold gear drops sell for a fortune, because heavy spenders turn them in to Supply Requests for Abyss Points. Even without gear, the box gives soul-bind fragments. ▶ 28:00
              2. 17
                Flip the Supply Request stock at weekly reset.Log in right as the weekly section resets and buy out the stock (white, green and blue gear, weapons especially), then resell it for about three times the price. There's no world market at launch and the stock differs by server, so demand stays high. ▶ 29:34

              The 1,000,000 Kinah daily cap is really a loot cap. It applies per character, on your faction's mainland only. Once you hit it, mobs stop dropping loot and only give pet souls. So farm mobs that give little Kinah per kill: you get more kills, and more gold-item drops (about 10–15 per cap), before you hit it. ▶ 0:38 ▶ 4:05

                  Never do this

                  The leveling mistakes veterans of the Korean and Taiwanese servers keep warning about.

                  • Shugo Festival before 45. Rewards scale with level; keys stack, so bank them.
                  • Opening story-dungeon chests. They cost Odyle Energy you'll want at 45.
                  • Enhancing leveling gear past +5. Extraction refunds stones, never the Kina you paid.
                  • Forgetting to slot specialties. They don't activate until you drag them into the slot.
                  • Action combat mode on a mana-hungry class. Target mode weaves the auto-attack that refills MP.
                  • Dissolving gold items. They reroll soul-bind stats later.
                  • Manastones on leveling gear. It's gone in days; the stones are worth a fortune.
                  • Crossing the map for side content. Do what's on your route; the full cleanup comes later.
                  • Auto-flying on low stamina. Stamina runs out, you drop, fall damage can kill.
